# Showcase audit — 2026-10-22

Audit of the first two daily desk-pass runs (2026-10-21, 2026-10-22): 20 countries, 204 findings. All records stay `review_status: unreviewed` until corrections are applied.

## Method

1. **Mechanical:** the runs changed only new country files, `queue.json` and `RUNLOG.md`; all 20 records pass `check_country.py`; queue and files agree.
2. **Coding read-through:** every finding read for pole, tier, reversal and confidence logic.
3. **Source verification:** four independent checkers, who had not seen how the findings were made, re-opened the cited sources for 107 findings — every reversal and every tier 4–5 finding (82), plus a random sample of 25 others.

## Coding problems (rules, not facts)

1. **Open-weight base models coded inconsistently.** SGP-009 (SEA-LION on Alibaba Qwen) is coded `prc`; KHM-008 and CHL-005 (on Meta Llama) and VNM-013 (on Nvidia Nemotron) are coded `domestic`. Same situation, opposite poles — biases towards PRC.
2. **State-owned, PRC-built infrastructure coded inconsistently.** BGD-006 (ZTE-built national data centre) is `prc`; CMR-003 (Huawei-equipped Camtel data centre) and COG-006 (Chinese-built Brazzaville data centre) are `domestic`. This is why Congo shows as 'leans sovereign'.
3. **Reversals that are not stack choices move the map.** Permit/environmental pauses (IDN-014 BDx, CHL-002 Google Cerrillos) and exits decided by the foreign company or by sanctions (BLR-005/006, MMR-005/006/007, CHN-002/008) subtract from a pole as if the country had chosen against it.
4. **Non-adoption events coded as adoption.** SGP-006 (prosecution over Nvidia chip diversion) adds US weight to Singapore; it is enforcement, not deployment.
5. **No recency or scale weighting.** A 2011 camera network in one small town (ARG-009) counts the same as a national contract. Together with old snapshots (ARG-001, PHL-001, BRN-001) this makes Argentina show as 'leans PRC'.
6. **Corroboration from unread headlines.** Several `corroborated: true` findings rest on a second source seen only as a search-result headline (ARG-003, AUS-001, AUS-004, CHN-007, PHL-002, MMR-006, MMR-007).
7. **Single S1 source forced to low confidence** (CHN-002, an SEC filing): the checker's corroboration rule caps it, which under-weights the strongest kind of source.

## Recommended changes

**Spec (before more runs, so the remaining 217 countries are coded consistently):**
- Models: a nationally fine-tuned open-weight model is `domestic`; record the base model and its pole in new fields `base_model` / `base_model_pole`.
- Infrastructure: controller is whoever operates and can update it; a foreign builder or lender is recorded in `builder` / `financier` fields, and becomes the controller only where it operates or maintains with access (as CMR-004 documents for Huawei at Camtel).
- Reversals: new required field `reversal_reason`: `geopolitical`, `sanctions`, `commercial`, `regulatory_environmental`. Only `geopolitical` and `sanctions` move the map.
- `corroborated: true` requires the run to have opened both sources; headline-only sources are listed but do not count.
- New field `scale`: `national`, `subnational`, `municipal`.

**Map colour rule:** halve findings dated before 2020 with no current-status confirmation; halve `municipal` findings; skip non-geopolitical reversals.

**Records:** apply the factual fixes in the table above and the recoding for problems 1–4, as a single corrections commit made in a supervised session (daily runs cannot edit existing records by design). Each corrected record keeps a `corrections` list naming this audit.
