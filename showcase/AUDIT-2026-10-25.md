## Method

1. **Mechanical.** All 140 records pass `check_country.py`. All 160 codes in `queue.json` `done` have a file, and every file is in `done`. RUNLOG entries since the last audit were read by the read-through agents (problems below).
2. **Coding read-through.** Ten desk-review agents with no web access read all 140 records (1,005 findings) against `SPEC.md` v0.2. They raised 391 issues; 24 were marked as unambiguously required by SPEC.
3. **Source verification.** 11 independent checkers, who did not write the findings, worked through their batches until the search budget ran out. They fetched only URLs from their own search results and no Chinese-hosted sites. Two checkers recorded one `retrieved_at` per batch rather than per fetch (CRI/DEU/GBR batch, SRB/TGO batch), so those timestamps are approximate.
4. **Corrections** were applied only to the 28 audited records. The only changes made were factual fixes backed by a page a checker read, `still_current` from checker evidence, newer information appended to `analyst_inference` with the prefix `Audit 2026-10-25:`, new sources the checkers opened (`opened: true`), and three pieces of recoding (listed below). Corroboration was upgraded only where this audit opened and added a second independent S1–S3 source, from a different host, with at least one S1/S2.

## Rule problems — proposals for Brian (not applied)

1. **Run size versus platform limits.** Brian's cap is 150 per run; `AUDIT_PROCEDURE.md` says 80; the guard allows 120 records per push; the procedure asks for one commit. A run is also limited to 200 web searches across all agents, which is enough for about 170 findings, or about 30 records at the current selection rule. *Proposal:* set the per-run cap to about 30 records, or keep 150 for read-through and split source verification across runs. Update the procedure's 80 to match, and allow checked findings to carry over between runs.
2. **Vendor and company press releases: S1 or S4?** Ericsson, Nokia, Google Press Corner and Business Wire releases are S1 in most records and S4 in others (GBR-008 against DEU-006, ZMB-007, UZB-007, TGO-005, KWT-002, BWA-005). The choice decides corroboration and the medium exception. *Proposal:* issuer releases about the issuer's own deals are S4; the counterparty government's notice or a filing is S1.
3. **Independence.** Echoes of one release or one wire story count as corroboration in many records (AGO-001/003, UGA-003, PER-001, COL-001, DNK-012, BHR-001, HND-001, LVA-003) and not in others. *Proposal:* sources that trace to one origin count once.
4. **"Single opened S1" medium exception.** It is unclear for two S1 pages, or S1 plus S3 sources that don't corroborate (PAK-002, MWI-003, URY-009, LTU-006). *Proposal:* say "every opened source that supports the finding is S1" or "at least one opened S1".
5. **Pole when the equipment vendor is unknown.** Records use the donor (BOL-002, DOM-004/005, TJK-002), the operator (HTI-001, AFG-004, RWA-004), the cable owner (FJI-003, TGO-004, DOM-007) or `null`. *Proposal:* `null`, with the owner, donor or operator in `financier`/`local_actor`.
6. **Must corroboration cover the pole-setting fact** (the vendor), not just the event? CHE-007, GRC-008, GIN-004, ERI-001, LCA-001.
7. **Reversals.** No default when no reason is given: DEU-012 regulatory, FIN-002 commercial, ITA-001 geopolitical. Operator Huawei swap-outs where security was cited are coded commercial (NOR-004, DNK-002, LUX-001). Exclusion laws with no vendor yet designated are tier 5 (POL-001, CAN-001) or tier 3 (BEL-003). Pre-2020 reversals are never halved (IRN-002/003). No rule removes duplicate reversals of the same equipment. *Proposal:* a security reason cited by the operator or government → geopolitical; no reason → commercial; exclusion laws score at the tier of the equipment actually removed; halve or confirm pre-2020 reversals.
8. **Non-instruments and model releases.** Sales pitches, exploratory talks and losing bids are given tiers (GAB-007, HND-005, FSM-001). Model releases range from tier 2 to tier 5 (GRC-006, FIN-010, IND-009, ISR-006).
9. **Global-layer overlap and non-AI facilities.** See recurring problems. *Proposal:* don't code hyperscaler facilities where the cloud layer already has a region; exclude crypto-mining and generic office software.
10. **Hong Kong and Macao.** SAR government sites (info.gov.hk, gcs.gov.mo) were fetched and used as S1; hard rule 4 may forbid that. The SAR = `domestic` convention is not in SPEC.
11. **Undefined terms:** `confidence: "high"`; "domestic" for foreign-owned local subsidiaries (UKR-008, KWT-005, ITA-011); the pole for EuroHPC AI factories (domestic in CZE-006/HUN-007, null in ROU-004, third in BGR-005, us in CYP-004); `base_model` for API-only use.
12. **Selection asymmetry.** Random sampling can corroborate one half of a paired finding but not the other (KEN-001/002, fixed by hand here). *Proposal:* a checker may add an opened page to sibling findings that the page also supports.
